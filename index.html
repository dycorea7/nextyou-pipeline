NextYou Content Pipeline

Operated by Dong Yeop Lee, individual creator

NextYou Content Pipeline is a private, single-user tool used by its operator to publish original video content to the operator's own YouTube channel. It is not distributed and has no other users.

What this is

This page is the public homepage for NextYou Content Pipeline, an automation workflow that assembles video content and, after personal review and approval, uploads it to the YouTube channel below.

Developed using YouTube API Services Uploads are published to youtube.com/@NextYouGlobal-i9w
